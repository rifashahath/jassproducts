# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Urban Indian consumers seeking authentic, clean Ayurvedic care for hair & skin issues (hard borewell water damage, dandruff, pollution, scalp flaking) without synthetic chemicals, sulphates, parabens, silicones, or artificial fragrances.

## Product Purpose

Deliver cold-pressed, single-origin botanical remedies formulated specifically for Indian environmental conditions (hard water minerals, coastal humidity, tropical heat). Success means verifiable relief (reduced flaking, restored tensile strength, balanced scalp sebum) paired with an uncompromised standard of ingredient transparency.

## Positioning

100% cold-pressed, wild-harvested botanicals with complete Latin-binomial ingredient disclosure and zero synthetic fillers or foaming agents (leveraging natural saponins from Shikakai & Reetha). A transparent apothecary approach that rejects mass-market D2C greenwashing and gimmicks.

## Operating Context

Daily hair, bath, and skin rituals in Indian households. Customers evaluate formulations based on authentic botanical purity, verified ingredient lists, and seamless delivery to their 6-digit Indian PIN code.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) web storefront with smooth single-page catalog navigation.
- Pan-India shipping with real-time 6-digit PIN code delivery checks.
- All product pricing denominated in Indian Rupees (INR, ₹) inclusive of GST.
- Payment options: Cash on Delivery (COD), UPI prepaid with automatic savings, credit/debit cards, and net banking.
- Free shipping on orders above ₹499.
- 7-day hassle-free return policy.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** Jass Products / Jass Ayurveda.
- **Voice:** Grounded, honest, editorial, and educational; strictly rejects AI marketing buzzwords ("miracle", "game-changer", "unleash", "elevate") and artificial urgency countdowns.
- **Botanical Integrity:** Full ingredient disclosure with Latin binomials on packaging and digital pages.

## Evidence on Hand

- Live product catalog spanning Hair Care, Soap & Bath, Skin Renewal, Health Care, Face Nourish, and Aromatherapy.
- Verified customer feedback from major Indian cities (Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Kochi).
- Clean 3D product imagery and packaging renders located in `/public/products/`.

## Product Principles

1. **Radical Botanical Transparency:** Always disclose exact herbs, cold-press extraction methods, and Latin binomials rather than vague proprietary blends.
2. **Formulated for Indian Realities:** Formulations directly solve regional concerns like borewell mineral buildup and humid scalp sweat.
3. **Respectful Commerce:** Honest INR pricing, clear COD availability, and realistic dispatch timelines with zero manipulative urgency patterns.
4. **Substance Over Slop:** Clear, legible typography, informative bento specs, and purposeful layouts over decorative visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA compliant text contrast across all light and warm-neutral surfaces.
- Large, comfortable reading sizes optimized for mobile viewports and diverse screen sizes across India.
- Clear touch targets for all interactive actions (cart additions, pincode checking, navigation).
